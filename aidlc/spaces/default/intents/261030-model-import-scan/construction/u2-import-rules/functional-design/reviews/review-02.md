> R-01 Reviewer note: BR7.1 and front door step 5 now compare the marker's importId with this import's own. A match finishes the import as stored with no email. The redrive branches are all defined: own marker, no marker (runs again), another importId (refused), outcome exists (nothing done).

> R-02 Reviewer note: BR8.2 adds deadline checks at every chunk of a download, before each retry (and the retry wait is cut short), and at every part of a multipart copy. It lists what cannot be interrupted, gives a 10-minute target, and names the 7-hour watchdog as the hard backstop. The "within minutes" claim is gone. The trickle bound cites u1 NFR1.7, which agrees with the u1 facts table.

> R-03 Reviewer note: rules.md lists BR4.1 among the u1 rules u2 changes, and BR7.1 states that the keep step's own check now fails only when a marker exists. The reverse table in traceability.json also names BR4.1.

BR8.2 exempts moves after the marker, and BR8.3 names the last check point just before the marker write. The state machine has no keeping-to-removing transition after the marker.
