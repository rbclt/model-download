### Checks That Held

- Alert (b): `RemovalIncomplete` in the stack is `import_outcome` and `removalComplete` is false, with no condition on the outcome. The claim is true.
- Lifecycle: `holding/` 2 days, `state/` 30 days, upload abort 1 day on both buckets, no expiry on the final bucket. The claim is true.
- Task role: object and multipart actions on `holding/*`, `state/*`, `models/*`, and `ListBucket` and `ListBucketMultipartUploads` on both buckets. The calls named for this Unit are covered, and no `s3:*` or `Resource: *` is present.
- Boolean `emailSent`: `logs.py` keeps booleans as JSON `true` and `false`, so `$.emailSent IS FALSE` works as it does for `removalComplete`. The field is not on the allow-list today and is added by this Unit (the design says so in `observability-design.md`). A stored outcome leaves it empty (entities.md), so alert (a) does not fire for it.
- Rollback: the u1 worker uses only `has_outcome` (a HEAD) on the outcome object. `read_outcome` and `read_record` are not called by the worker or coordinator, and the existing counter object `emails-<date>.json` keeps its format, so a rolled-back u1 build tolerates u2 objects. The claim is true.
- Numbers (20 notices, 30 failure emails, 200 findings, 20 leftovers listed, 3 tries for the marker, 10 minutes) and the no-expiry statement for unfinished files in the final bucket match `scalability-design.md` and `reliability-design.md`. The pipeline's choice to keep real events' 20 emails a day by not sending bad events in the smoke check is consistent.

### Suggestions

- `EmailNotSent` "by role": the stack's `_count_filter` has no dimension, so a per-role split needs a metric dimension on the role field or two filters. Say which.
- u1's smoke check says the holding bucket must be empty, which also needs holding-bucket list access that the same role description does not give. Settle this together with the smoke-check permission finding.

### Summary

Three Major findings block the design: the alert (a) change collides with the per-outcome count metric it is built on, the new smoke-check reads need permissions that the design says it does not add, and the deadline-stop metric cannot be produced from the existing filter. The remaining claims about the stack (alert (b), lifecycle rules, task role, rollback) held up.
