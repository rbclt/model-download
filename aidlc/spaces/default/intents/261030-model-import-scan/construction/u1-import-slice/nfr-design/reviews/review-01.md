### Summary

The design chooses workable AWS parts, and Q2 B and Q4 A answer the two carried-over findings in principle. Four Major gaps remain: a crashed or stuck message blocks the whole queue for 7.5 hours with no release and no message life cycle, the closed role list refuses the state objects the design depends on, the completion marker is not protected against partial removal, and the 50-emails limit cannot be built or alarmed as written. These must be settled before Infrastructure Design.

Verdict: NOT-READY
