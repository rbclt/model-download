### Summary

The set covers NFR1 to NFR6 and follows the owner's answers Q1 to Q8, including SES in place of SNS, but four gaps would make a developer guess or build something that cannot work: the permissions in NFR3.4 cannot be set as written, u1 has no time bound or recovery for a dead run, the SES sandbox precondition behind Q8 is open, and nothing limits parallel or repeated events in u1. Fix those and the measurement gaps, and it is ready to hand to NFR Design.
