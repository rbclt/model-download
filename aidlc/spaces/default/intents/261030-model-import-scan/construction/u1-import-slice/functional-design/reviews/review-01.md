### Summary

The traceability and the story split to u2 are correct, and the happy path and the main failure paths are clear. Four Major gaps stop a developer from building without guessing: an empty repository is stored as a model, the errors that happen during removal or the email have no defined end, the weight header and text-read checks cannot be tested, and untrusted names have constraints but no rule. The Minor findings cover Q4, the move failure, retry classes, limits, and the invalid event. The ignored class and the any-finding-fails rule are accepted by the owner and are not findings against the design.
