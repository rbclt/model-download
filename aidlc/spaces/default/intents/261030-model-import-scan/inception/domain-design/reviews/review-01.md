### Summary

The catalogue is well-formed, traceable to all seven stories, and consistent with Q1 and Q2. Two Major gaps should be weighed before approval. The rejected-event and already-stored emails are not covered by FailureNotifier and contradict ADR-005, and the coordinator-centred design does not say how the 6-hour stop or a coordinator crash still reaches exactly one outcome. The Minor items are small edits to entity shapes and ADR wording.
